## Full description (up to 4000 characters)
Uzbekistan on your phone: Registan Square and the domes of Samarkand, the minarets of Bukhara, the fortress walls of Khiva, Tashkent at night, the Chimgan mountains and Charvak, oriental bazaars, ceramics and ornaments — plus beautiful greeting cards to send to family and friends on WhatsApp.

SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
• New wallpapers and cards are added every day
• The feed mixes pictures from all categories and shows them in a new order every time you open it
• Fresh pictures go straight to the top of the feed

GREETING CARDS FOR WHATSAPP
• Ready-made cards for Navruz, Ramazon Hayit, Qurbon Hayit, New Year, March 8, Independence Day, Teachers and Mentors Day and Constitution Day
• Juma Muborak, good morning, good night and birthday cards — in Uzbek and Russian
• Add a name or your own greeting right on the card, choose the text colour and size
• Send to WhatsApp with one tap
• We remind you a day before the holiday that the cards are ready

HIGH-QUALITY WALLPAPERS
• Categories: nature, animals, architecture, religion, traditions
• Set on the home screen, the lock screen or both at once
• Auto-change: a new picture every day from your favorites or from the whole collection
• Save to your phone gallery and share with friends

EASY TO USE
• Favorites: save the pictures you like with one tap on the heart
• Search by words: "Samarkand", "mosque", "Navruz"
• Swipe down to close a picture, just like in social apps
• Light and dark theme, or follow the system
• Three languages: Uzbek, Russian and English

Photos from Wikimedia Commons are published with the author and licence credited.
