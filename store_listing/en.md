## Full description (up to 4000 characters)
Kazakhstan on your phone: the Trans-Ili Alatau mountains, Charyn Canyon, the Kolsai Lakes, Burabay, the endless steppe, Astana and Almaty, mosques and mausoleums — plus beautiful greeting cards to send to family and friends on WhatsApp.

SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
• New wallpapers and cards are added every day
• The feed mixes pictures from all categories and shows them in a new order each time you open it
• Fresh pictures go straight to the top of the feed

GREETING CARDS FOR WHATSAPP
• Ready-made cards for Nauryz, Oraza Ait, Kurban Ait, New Year, March 8, Victory Day, Capital City Day, Republic Day and Independence Day
• Friday blessings, good morning, good night and birthday cards in Kazakh and Russian
• Add a name or your own greeting right on the card, choose the text color and size
• Send to WhatsApp with one tap
• We remind you a day before a holiday that the cards are ready

HIGH-QUALITY WALLPAPERS
• Categories: nature, animals, architecture, religion, traditions
• Set on the home screen, the lock screen, or both
• Auto-change: a new picture every day from your favorites or from the whole collection
• Save to your phone's gallery and share with friends

EASY TO USE
• Favorites: save the pictures you like with one tap on the heart
• Search by word: "mountains", "mosque", "Nauryz"
• Swipe down to close a picture, just like in social apps
• Light and dark theme, or follow the system
• Three languages: Kazakh, Russian and English

Photos from Wikimedia Commons are published with the author and license credited.
